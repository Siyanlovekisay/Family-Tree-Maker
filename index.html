Why Family Trees Matter

Every family is a story that started long before us. Behind each name is someone who worked, sacrificed, laughed, and loved so that the next generation could have a better life. But when no one writes those names down, they slowly fade to everyone's memory, and one day nobody remembers who they were.

A family tree keeps them from being forgotten. It tells your children where they come from, shows you who you carry in your blood and your habits, and gives every member, from the oldest lola to the youngest baby, a place that says: you belong, and you are remembered.

Start with one name today. Add the people who raised you. Ask your grandparents for their stories while they can still tell them.
